Privacy Policy

What is collected, why, where it lives, and how to get it back or get rid of it.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Who runs this site

Finance Fundamentals is operated by an individual based in India, who is the data fiduciary for the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. For any question or request about your data, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

What is collected

You can use the calculators without an account. Nothing you type is stored anywhere unless you choose to sign in and save it.

If you create an account

  • Email and password, or — if you sign in with Google — your name, email address and profile picture from that Google account. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to this site.

If you save a calculation

  • What you called the purchase, its price, and the figures you entered: liquid savings, essential monthly expenses, take-home income, existing debt payments, monthly savings target, and the purchase horizon.
  • The resulting score and its classification, and when you saved it.

If you save details to your profile

  • The recurring figures you choose to store — savings, expenses, income, debt payments and savings target — so they can be filled in for you next time.

If you send feedback

  • Your message, any star rating, the page you sent it from, and — if you are signed in — your email address and account identifier.

Automatically

  • Google Analytics records page visits, approximate location derived from your IP address, and general device and browser information. This is used to understand how the site is used in aggregate.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps your light/dark theme choice, whether the calculator sidebar is collapsed, and your display name and picture so the page does not flicker while loading. This stays on your device.
  • The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs.

What is never collected

No bank account numbers, card details, PAN, Aadhaar or other government identifiers, and no connection to any bank or financial institution. The figures you enter are numbers you type — this site cannot see your actual accounts. Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

Why it is collected

Account data exists so you can sign in. Saved calculations and profile details exist only to provide the features you asked for — history and prefilled inputs. Feedback is used to improve the site. Analytics is used to understand usage in aggregate. Where the law requires consent, your act of creating an account and saving data is that consent, and you may withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

Where it is stored

Account information and saved data are held in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Analytics is processed by Google Analytics. The site is served by GitHub Pages. These providers may process and store data on servers outside India.

How long it is kept

Saved calculations and profile details are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting your account removes them immediately and permanently. Feedback messages are kept separately so they remain useful after an account is gone; ask at [CONTACT EMAIL] if you want yours removed. Analytics data follows Google's own retention period.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask for access to your data, correction of anything inaccurate, and erasure. In practice:

  • Access and correction — your saved calculations and details are visible and editable while signed in, on the calculator and My details pages.
  • Erasure — delete individual calculations from your history, or delete your entire account and everything attached to it from My details. This cannot be undone.
  • Anything else — including a copy of your data or a complaint — write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS, authentication is handled by Google Firebase, and access rules restrict each account's saved data to that account. No service can promise perfect security, and this one does not.

Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18, and accounts should not be created by or for children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and it will be removed.

Changes

This policy may change as the site does. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
