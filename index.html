Affordability Index

A single number that tells you whether a purchase fits your finances

The Formula

The Fundamental Idea
AI =
Safe Savings + (Monthly Surplus × T) Purchase Price

Where Safe Savings = S − 6E and Monthly Surplus = I − E − D − M

The Detailed Formula
AI =
(S − 6E) + (I − E − D − M)T P
P Purchase price
S Liquid savings
E Essential monthly expenses
I Monthly take-home income
D Existing monthly debt payments
M Monthly savings / investment target
T Purchase horizon, in months
