Why Finance Fundamentals

Money deserves better education

We wanted to make financial literacy easy and accessible to everyone around the world. Whether we like it or not, money is a part of everyone's life — yet our education system rarely teaches us how to understand it or make it work for us. We can spend years learning Algebra and Science, but somehow never learn the basics of managing money. Our mission is to help everyday people understand money, make smarter financial decisions, and answer one important question: How can I make better decisions with my money?

FAQ

Common questions

Is this financial advice?

No. Finance Fundamentals is a set of calculators, not a licensed advisor — it turns the numbers you enter into a clear score, not a recommendation to buy or not buy.

Is my data saved anywhere?

Each calculation you run is saved to your account's history so you can look back on it later — visible only to you, and you can delete any entry at any time.

Is it free to use?

Yes — the Affordability Index and every calculator added after it are free to use.

What calculators are coming next?

More are in progress. They'll appear alongside the Affordability Index on the same calculator page.
